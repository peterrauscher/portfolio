ADHD brain: thoughts context-switch like a hot CPU. Anyone who codes knows that tax.

Docs, videos, an ad on the train — I constantly hit something I want to chase later. I used to open a hundred tabs, bookmark everything, or trust I'd remember to Google it. That fell apart.
